# Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data {service} collects, why, how long it keeps it and the rights you have under the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (PDPL) and its implementing regulations.

## Who we are

{service} provides a video subscription service for content it owns or is licensed to show. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

- Address: {seller_address}
- Email: {support_email}
- Phone: {support_phone}

## What we collect

- **Account data:** your name, email address, phone number, preferred language and password (stored only as a one-way hash).
- **Subscription and payment data:** your plan, invoices and payment status. Card payments are handled by our payment providers; we never receive or store your full card number.
- **Viewing data:** what you watch, where you stopped, your list, ratings and watch history, so we can resume playback and recommend titles.
- **Device and security data:** the devices and TV apps you use, IP addresses, the approximate country and city derived from them, sign-in attempts and stream sessions. We use these to protect your account, enforce your plan's stream and device limits and prevent abuse.
- **Messages:** what you send to our support team.

## Why we use it

- To provide the service you subscribed to (performance of a contract).
- To bill you and keep invoices as tax law requires (legal obligation).
- To keep accounts and content secure and to detect account sharing beyond your plan (legitimate interest).
- To send service messages such as receipts, renewal reminders and security alerts. Marketing messages are sent only with your consent, and you can stop them at any time.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for these purposes. Watch history, sign-in records and stream sessions are deleted or anonymised automatically after the periods set in our retention settings. Invoices are kept for the period tax law requires. When you close your account we delete or anonymise your data, except what we must keep by law.

## Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the service (hosting, payment processing, email and messaging) under contracts that protect it, and with authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

Some providers may process data outside the Kingdom. When that happens, we transfer it only as the PDPL allows and with appropriate safeguards.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- be informed about how your personal data is processed;
- access your personal data and obtain a copy of it;
- ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- ask us to delete data we no longer need;
- withdraw consent you have given, without affecting earlier processing.

To use these rights, contact us at {support_email}. You can also file a complaint with the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

## Security

We protect personal data with encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Children

The service is for adults. Children may watch under the supervision of the account holder, who controls the account.

## Changes

We may update this policy. We will show the new version here and tell you about significant changes before they take effect.
